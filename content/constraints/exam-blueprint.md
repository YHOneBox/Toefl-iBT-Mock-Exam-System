# TOEFL iBT Enhanced Exam Blueprint (2026)

Generate original practice items that match this structure. Do not copy official ETS items.

## Sequence and timing

Reading (adaptive) then Listening (adaptive) then Writing (linear) then Speaking (linear). No scheduled break. Directions are untimed.

## Reading (~50 items, ~30 minutes)

Two-stage adaptive. Module 1 is the router (~35 items, 18-21 minutes). Module 2 is lower or upper (~15 items, 9 minutes).

Task types:

1. Complete the Words: academic paragraph 70-100 words. First sentence intact. After that, delete the second half of every second word until there are 10 gaps. Test taker types the missing letters. Gaps must be uniquely recoverable from context.
2. Read in Daily Life: 15-150 words. Formats: email, notice, sign, menu, social post, schedule, text-message chain, form. 2-3 multiple-choice questions per text.
3. Read an Academic Passage: about 200 words, introductory university level, no specialized background required. 5 questions covering factual, negative factual, vocabulary, sentence simplification, reference, inference, rhetorical purpose, insert-text.

Question rules:

- Correct answers paraphrase the text. Do not copy the passage wording into the correct option.
- Distractors reuse words from the text but twist relationships, exaggerate, or reverse the claim.
- Each item is worth 1 raw point.

## Listening (~47 items, ~29 minutes)

Two-stage adaptive. Module 1 ~32 items, 18 minutes. Module 2 ~15 items: lower 7 minutes or upper 11 minutes.

Accents: US/Canada, UK, Australia. Mix genders. Academic talks slightly slower than conversations. Audio is played once. The Listen and Choose stem is heard only, never printed.

Task types:

1. Listen and Choose a Response: brief campus question or statement. Four written replies. Choose the socially appropriate response.
2. Listen to a Conversation: 35-100 words, two speakers, campus/daily topics, 2 questions.
3. Listen to an Announcement: 40-85 words, campus notice, 2 questions.
4. Listen to an Academic Talk: 175-250 words, 4 questions.

Question intents: main idea, factual, inference, purpose, method, attitude.

## Writing (12 items, 23 minutes)

1. Build a Sentence: 10 items, 6 minutes. Short exchange plus scrambled words/phrases. Reorder into one grammatical, contextual sentence.
2. Write an Email: 7 minutes. Scenario with a communicative goal (request, refuse, complain, thank, arrange).
3. Write for an Academic Discussion: 10 minutes. Professor post plus two student posts. Write a relevant, elaborated contribution.

## Speaking (11 items, ~8 minutes)

1. Listen and Repeat: 7 sentences in one campus/academic scenario. Visual path advances. Record once. Timers: 8, 8, 10, 10, 10, 12, 12 seconds. Sentences get longer and more complex.
2. Take an Interview: 4 questions, 45 seconds each. Progress from personal fact, to reaction, to opinion, to a broader policy question.

## Scoring notes for generated metadata

Tag every item with taskType, cefr (A1-C2), module (m1 or m2 or linear), skill, answerKey, and rationale.
