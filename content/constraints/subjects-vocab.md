# Academic subjects, campus contexts, and item-writing rules

## Academic disciplines for reading passages and academic talks

- History and Anthropology: ancient civilizations, archaeology, societal change
- Life Sciences: biology, ecology, biomes, plant and animal distribution
- Physical Sciences: geology, oceanography, climate, properties of matter
- Social Sciences: urban development, community, social integration
- Art and Music: art history, acoustics of performance spaces, visual culture
- Business and Economics: management, supply chains, industrial history

Passages and talks must be introductory. No specialized prior knowledge.

## Campus and daily-life contexts

Use for Read in Daily Life, Listen and Choose, conversations, announcements, emails, Listen and Repeat scenarios:

- Academic logistics: syllabi, registration, grades, library, tech bar
- Campus events and facilities: dining, dorms, rec center, wellness, contests
- Student interaction: study groups, clubs, group projects

## Vocabulary expectations

Academic words should be inferable from context, roots, and affixes. Mix academic and everyday language. Useful high-frequency academic items include: locomotion, immense, exceed, impromptu, figures, prized, subsequent, nevertheless, consequently, furthermore.

Cohesive devices to use in texts and to test in insert-text / method items:

- Contrast: however, nevertheless, instead, by contrast
- Conclusion: therefore, consequently, as a result, hence
- Addition: additionally, furthermore, moreover
- Sequence: initially, subsequently, next
- Example: for example, for instance, such as
- Similarity: similarly, likewise

## Generation constraints

1. Distractors must use words found in the stimulus but misrepresent relationships, exaggerate, or state the opposite.
2. Correct options must paraphrase, not quote.
3. Academic reading about 200 words. Academic listening scripts 175-250 words. Daily-life texts 15-150 words. Conversations 35-100 words. Announcements 40-85 words.
4. Complete the Words: 70-100 words, 10 uniquely recoverable gaps.
5. Keep content culturally neutral and appropriate for a general adult audience.
