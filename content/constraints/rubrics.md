# Constructed-response rubrics (0-5)

Use these official-style trait descriptions when scoring. Return JSON only.

## Write an Email

5 Fully successful: effective, clearly expressed, consistent language facility; elaboration supports the communicative purpose; syntactic variety and precise word choice; appropriate social conventions; almost no errors except timed-writing slips.
4 Generally successful: mostly effective and easily understood; adequate elaboration; appropriate word choice; mostly appropriate conventions; few errors.
3 Partially successful: generally accomplishes the task; some unclear parts; moderate range; noticeable errors in structure, word form, idioms, or conventions.
2 Mostly unsuccessful: attempt is mostly ineffective; limited or irrelevant elaboration; limited syntax/vocabulary; accumulated errors.
1 Unsuccessful: ineffective or nearly unintelligible; telegraphic language; serious frequent errors; or mostly borrowed from the prompt.
0 Blank, off topic, not English, copied, or unconnected.

## Write for an Academic Discussion

5 Relevant, very clearly expressed contribution; well-elaborated explanations or examples; varied syntax and precise vocabulary; almost no errors.
4 Relevant and easily understood; adequately elaborated; variety of structures; few errors.
3 Mostly relevant and understandable; some missing or unclear elaboration; some variety; noticeable errors.
2 Attempted contribution; poorly elaborated or only partly relevant; limited range; accumulated errors.
1 Ineffective; few coherent ideas; severely limited range; serious errors; or mostly borrowed language.
0 Blank, off topic, not English, copied, or unconnected.

## Listen and Repeat

5 Exact repetition and fully intelligible.
4 Meaning preserved but not exact: minor function-word changes, one related content-word swap, tense/number slip, or two transposed words; one or two words may be slightly unclear.
3 Essentially full sentence but meaning drifts; several function-word problems or a changed content word; occasional intelligibility issues.
2 Missing a large part or highly inaccurate; fragmentary; low intelligibility.
1 Very little of the prompt or largely unintelligible, but still an attempt.
0 No response, no English, or entirely unconnected.

## Take an Interview

5 Fully addresses the question; clear and fluent; on topic and well elaborated; natural pace; intelligible pronunciation and intonation; range of accurate grammar and vocabulary.
4 Addresses the question and is reasonably clear; elaborated but connectors may be weak; generally good pace; meaning not blocked; grammar/vocabulary adequate.
3 Addresses the question with limited elaboration or clarity; choppy pace; intelligibility sometimes affected; limited range restricts precision.
2 Attempt is not supported in a meaningful or intelligible way; little relevant elaboration; limited intelligibility; very limited range.
1 Minimally addresses the question; mostly unintelligible or isolated words.
0 No response, no English, or entirely unconnected.
